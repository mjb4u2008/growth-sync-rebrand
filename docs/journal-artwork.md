## Final prompts

### A new kind of company

Create one original wide editorial cover image for GrowthSync's essay 'A new kind of company'. Use case: stylized-concept, rendered like an extraordinary physical sculpture photographed for a design monograph. Scene: a precise, open architectural lattice made of many slender matte graphite beams and folded graphite planes. It begins as one small coherent structure on the lower left and extends into an expansive but legible series of connected spaces toward the right. A single vivid orange filament threads through the architecture, connecting distant parts while the actual structure stays black. The object feels handmade by a meticulous industrial designer, with subtle machining and real material edges, not a computer network icon. The concept is a small organization gaining new capabilities on a shared foundation. Bright neutral almost-white studio void and soft directional daylight, beautiful deep but soft shadows, restrained black-white-orange palette, generous negative space at top, unusual oblique perspective, strong silhouette, sophisticated calm confidence and possibility. Full-bleed landscape 16:9 composition, no words, no letters, no labels, no logos, no typography, no people, no globes, no glowing neon, no circuit-board cliches, no stock corporate visual, no decorative UI. This must be a materially convincing three-dimensional art photograph, not an infographic or wireframe illustration.

### The end of starting over

Original wide editorial art for a thoughtful founder essay about a company retaining its memory as its software changes. A meticulous physical sculpture photographed for a design monograph: seven translucent smoked-glass planes stand upright on a neutral white studio floor, staggered in depth like pages. Each plane has different extremely fine etched geometric paths, no text or numbers. One single vivid orange cord passes continuously through precisely aligned round holes in all the planes, connecting the layers. A few dark graphite blocks anchor the structure. Past and present visible together through the glass, clear continuity instead of a random data pile. Oblique close perspective with beautiful refractions, soft natural daylight, delicate shadows, materially convincing glass and matte graphite, restrained black-white-orange palette, sophisticated calm and curiosity. Landscape 16:9. Leave negative space at top. No letters, logos, words, UI, brain shapes, neon glow, server racks, circuit-board cliches, people or extra colors. This should look like a commissioned art object made by an industrial designer and physically photographed, not a diagram.

### Beyond the session

Original wide editorial art for a thoughtful startup engineering essay about work continuing across the end of an AI session. A sophisticated small physical sculpture photographed on a bright neutral-white studio floor: two separated low platforms of dark graphite, with a third narrow dark island in the gap, and a single vivid orange tensioned cable or thin tubular path traversing the entire distance, continuing confidently across open space. The first platform supports an intricate small cluster of angular folded graphite forms, the last supports a different larger arrangement; the orange line stays continuous though the forms change. Precise engineering, architectural model, unexpected beautiful sculptural composition. Strong lateral visual movement, viewpoint slightly above, soft clear daylight with long delicate shadows, materials tactile and physically credible. Restrained graphite, orange, neutral-white palette. Landscape 16:9, full bleed but generous breathing space. No text, lettering, numbers, people, arrows, logos, UI, glowing neon, gradients, spaceships, data-center cliches. Make it feel like original art commissioned by an ambitious, thoughtful technology founder.
