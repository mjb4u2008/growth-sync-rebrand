# Michael Voice

## Core Read

Make the writing sound like Michael thinking out loud as a founder who has already done the work. The voice is direct, specific, impatient with filler, and excited by market structure. It should feel like a sharp memo someone could send to investors, operators, or a team channel.

## Operating Rules

- State the claim plainly, then put the data or concrete proof immediately after it.
- Prefer short declarative sentences. Cut throat-clearing, hedging, and repeated setup.
- Do not repeat the headline as the opening sentence.
- Move fast from macro trend to company implication to GrowthSync thesis.
- Use numbers, company examples, market transitions, and causal language.
- Separate facts from interpretation, but do not sound timid when the pattern is obvious.
- Avoid sounding like content marketing. No generic "explores," "unlock," "delve," or SEO filler.
- Avoid taking shots at companies. Frame hard examples as market evidence, case studies, or pressure from changing distribution economics.
- No em dashes.

## Preferred Shape

1. Lead with the shift in one or two sentences.
2. Explain the old model.
3. Show what broke, using specific data.
4. Use company examples as evidence, not gossip.
5. Name the new model clearly.
6. Show why small operators or early movers are already winning.
7. Tie the thesis back to GrowthSync as the operating layer for the new market.

## Sentence Moves

Prefer:

- "Social media is no longer just where brands create awareness."
- "The old playbook was simple."
- "This was e-commerce 1.0."
- "The website is not dead. The website is demoted."
- "The tailwinds are obvious."

Avoid:

- "The convergence is happening" as a repeated first sentence under that same title.
- "This is not about X or Y" when the paragraph can simply state what the example proves.
- Long paragraphs that re-explain a point already made.
- Soft transitions like "at the same time" when causality is clearer.

## Revision Checklist

Before finishing a Michael-voice piece:

- Cut the first sentence of any paragraph if the second sentence is stronger.
- Check whether two adjacent paragraphs make the same point. Merge or delete one.
- Replace vague market language with a company, metric, or mechanism.
- Make the GrowthSync section bigger than a CTA. It should explain why GrowthSync exists now.
- Keep the final CTA simple and direct.
