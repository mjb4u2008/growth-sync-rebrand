# Writing Style

GrowthSync blog posts should feel like a sharp founder or operator wrote them fast, not like content marketing software assembled them.

## Voice

- Opinionated, specific, and plainspoken
- Short paragraphs
- Strong point of view
- Concrete numbers when available
- No em dashes
- No filler words, hype copy, or generic SEO language

## Title Rules

Good titles usually do one of these:

- say what changed, then why it matters
- take a hard position
- expose a mistake most brands are making
- connect a platform move to a revenue implication

Examples from the site:

- `Instagram Just Copied TikTok Shop's Playbook. Here's What That Means for Every E-Commerce Brand`
- `The TikTok Halo Effect Is Real, and Most Brands Aren't Maximizing It`
- `TikTok Has a Reputation Problem. They're Spending Millions to Fix It.`

## Excerpt Rules

- 1 or 2 sentences
- under roughly 180 characters when possible
- specific enough that the grid card feels worth clicking
- no vague "explores" language

## Body Structure

The common shape is:

1. A strong opening paragraph in the larger lead style
2. Supporting paragraphs that frame the argument
3. `h2` sections that walk the reader through the thesis
4. A clear GrowthSync tie-back near the end
5. A `Sources` section when the piece uses timely claims, launch news, or hard numbers

`Rod Henley`

## Category and Tags

Prefer categories that already exist in `src/data/blogPosts.tsx`.

Common safe choices:

- `Social Commerce`
- `Strategy`
- `Company`
- `Product`
- `AI`

Tags should usually be 3 to 5 items, and they should feel like terms a sales call or search query would actually use.
