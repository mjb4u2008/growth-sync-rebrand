# GrowthSync public website

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective customer brands, SMB founders and investors arriving to understand the company or open a conversation. The homepage is a first impression; the journal holds longer writing.

## Product Purpose

Introduce GrowthSync as the company building a maintained AI operating foundation for businesses. Core is the first product; commerce is the starting market. One short hero and one brand inquiry action, with the existing journal and legal routes preserved.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, kinetic, precise. Mike's November 1 brief asks for minimal, cryptic, exceptionally clean, with a playful mouse interaction and the confidence of an incognito technology company. The early Natural reference describes the atmosphere, not its current long product page.

## Anti-references

Long SaaS landing pages, feature cards, logo walls, fake activity, unsupported proof claims, social-commerce-only positioning, and decorative terminal language. Historical cream/chrome marketing directions are superseded for the new homepage.

## Design Principles

- One memorable object; one clear invitation.
- Let a visitor discover the form through movement.
- Keep the company story broad and the copy short.
- Preserve a practical, honest intake beneath the intriguing surface.
- Review locally before deciding how to replace the current Framer site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA text/control contrast, standard cursor, visible keyboard focus, native dialog, reduced-motion and pause behavior. Touch and keyboard can manipulate the object. Normal desktop and phone compositions fit one screen; short screens, zoomed text and the open form may scroll rather than clip.

## November 1 refinement

Mike explicitly approved the simplicity of the homepage and requested that the blog become minimal too. Carry the same restrained visual language into both the journal index and the article reader, preserving the writing and its URLs.
