# GrowthSync minimal homepage

## Direction

A visitor opens a link on a laptop in daylight and finds a small mathematical object floating in a bright, empty room. Its surface responds to their hand. The page feels intentionally unfinished in what it reveals, completely finished in how it behaves.

## Palette and type

Restrained: neutral white (#fafafa), graphite (#161719), readable gray (#5c5d60), and a small tangerine filament carried over from GrowthSync's identity. CSS uses OKLCH tokens. Hanken Grotesk preserves the existing brand's typography; 400–500 weight for the statement, 650 for the wordmark. No editorial serif or technical monospace.

## Composition

Wordmark left, Journal right. A centered, interactive woven loop above a two-line statement and a single dark inquiry button. Quiet legal links and motion control at the bottom. No homepage sections below the fold. The layout grows naturally on short/zoomed screens.

## Motion

The woven loop slowly turns. Pointer movement tips it; dragging and arrow keys turn it; clicking or pressing Enter reshapes it. One orange filament makes the topology readable. Pause, reduced-motion, hidden tabs and offscreen state suspend continuous drawing. Reduced-motion manipulation changes the still frame directly.

## Inquiry

Native modal dialog, explicitly labeled controls, required name/email/company/website, optional context. Client and server validation, retained values on failure, one in-flight request, bounded waits and a confirmed capture receipt before success. Analytics cannot reverse a confirmed capture.

## Existing routes

The journal, article and legal page contents retain their existing styling and paths. Their historical copy is outside this homepage redesign.

## Journal, approved direction November 1

The homepage's simplicity carries into the journal: one unadorned dated list, low-contrast dividing rules, a few text filters and a small search field. No windows, file icons, status bars, category pills or cards. The reader is one comfortable text column with an optional contents disclosure and simple related links. Hanken Grotesk stays consistent with the homepage.

The editorial reset keeps eight substantial pieces. Show a short excerpt in the index so readers can choose an argument, with compact Perspectives, Studies and Team filters. Case studies distinguish reported investment from our interpretation. Source links sit with the claim and in a plain source list; adapted personal essays carry a quiet provenance note.

## Selected hero — November 1

The homepage uses Orbit: a moving field of black and orange particles, with the two-line headline “The shape of / what’s next.” This supersedes the woven loop and foundation headline as the default. Keep the same restraint, whitespace, typography, inquiry action, and motion accessibility behavior.

## Founder journal revision

The index leads with one illustrated thesis, then the chronological list. Retain the restrained palette and Hanken Grotesk. Original graphite-and-orange sculptural artwork explores connection, memory, and continuity; the existing Tanner portrait supplies the human element. Figures explain operating mechanisms with readable labels, not simulated product UI. The reader has a generous title, a short deck, visible original/revision dates, and a 720px text column; art can extend wider. The existing inquiry is offered separately after the argument and portaled outside journal styles.
