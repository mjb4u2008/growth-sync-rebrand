GrowthSync Brand Guide
======================

https://growthsync.com/brand

Core assets
-----------
- Mark: /brand/growthsync-mark.svg
- Wordmark: /brand/growthsync-wordmark.svg
- Wordmark (light): /brand/growthsync-wordmark-light.svg
- Lockup: /brand/growthsync-lockup.svg

Core colors
-----------
- Graphite: #030712
- Teal: #14B8A6
- Cyan: #06B6D4
- Violet: #8B5CF6
- Pink: #EC4899
- Cloud: #F9FAFB

Typography
----------
- Display: Outfit
- Body and UI: Inter

Font sources
------------
- Outfit: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Outfit
- Inter: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter

Usage notes
-----------
- Use the full lockup on first mention.
- Use the mark in small surfaces like avatars and favicons.
- Keep generous clear space around the logo.
- Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, or add effects to the mark.
- Use the violet-to-pink gradient sparingly for statement moments.
