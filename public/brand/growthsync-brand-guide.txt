----------

Font sources
------------
- Hanken Grotesk: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Hanken+Grotesk
- Inter: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter

Usage notes
-----------
- Use the full lockup on first mention.
- Use the icon/mark in small surfaces like avatars and favicons.
- Keep generous clear space around the logo.
- Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, or add effects to the mark.
- Use the tangerine sphere with the GrowthSync wordmark as the primary lockup.
